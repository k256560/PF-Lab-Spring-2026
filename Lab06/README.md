OBJECTIVES: 
Loops are used to efficiently iterate through data structures without repetitive code, ensuring tasks are performed systematically.
They execute based on specific conditions and stop once those conditions are no longer met.  
Loops simplify complex operations by organizing repeated actions into clear and structured blocks.  
They support continuous monitoring in event-driven or real-time applications.  
Additionally, loops provide flexible control over program flow, allowing execution to start, continue, or terminate based on logical conditions.
